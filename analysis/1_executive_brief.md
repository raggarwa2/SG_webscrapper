# Executive Brief: Positioning MyACUVUE in Singapore

Evidence as of 1 October 2026. Four independent Research runs triangulated with scraped consumer, retailer and app data. Source IDs in [00_source_log.md](00_source_log.md); full evidence in [2_deep_dive_report.md](2_deep_dive_report.md). Out of scope: regulation, market sizing, the "eligible trial consumer" definition, tactics and message copy.

## Headline findings

1. **MyACUVUE leads on structure, trails on experience.** It runs the only always-on, app-based membership in Singapore (points, VIP tier, store-linked rewards). CooperVision and Bausch + Lomb run time-limited campaigns; Alcon runs a 12-month e-stamp card; Olens has a nascent app (R2-C-13/14/16/17, R4 grid; High for mechanics). The app is rated 2.4 on the Singapore App Store (70 ratings) and 3.32 on Google Play, and reviews describe OTP failures, update breakage and points tied to one store (SC-APP, R4-C-04; Medium). Prevalence is unknown.
2. **The bigger leak may be earlier than the app.** Points accrue only at the member's chosen participating store (High). Wearers describe buying in Johor Bahru, online or elsewhere, where registering earns nothing (R4-C-12, R3-C-21, SC-RD; Medium, anecdotal). J&J's retail value share still rose from 34% to 36% in 2025 (R2-C-01; Medium), so the size of this leak is not established.
3. **Price is the most contested dimension.** ACUVUE sits S$15 to S$25 per box above PRECISION1 and clariti at the same retailer (R2; Medium), wearers benchmark cross-border and online prices, and Bausch + Lomb is the most promotional (R3-C-06; SC-PRD; Medium). No price territory is ownable on this evidence.
4. **Olens holds the appearance end of the market alone.** It has the most social attention (43% of YouTube comments; 39% of brand-relevant Xiaohongshu likes) and search interest similar to Acuvue, yet no official Singapore channel (SC-YT, SC-XHS, SC-GT, R2-C-25; Medium for attention, not sales).
5. **J&J does have a myopia-control product in Singapore** (Abiliti 1-Day since December 2022; High), contradicting one run's gap statement. CooperVision leads on evidence strength (MiSight; Medium); Abiliti's evidence is sponsor-sourced (Low).
6. **Scraped brand comparisons mostly fail audit.** Lazada reviews (98% five-star, three brands only), Google Maps (no brand field, 95% four- or five-star), YouTube and Reddit brand tags, and small Facebook, Instagram and forum samples cannot rank brands. The scraped findings that hold up concern friction types, price and channel talk, and Olens attention.

## Positioning implications (not actions)

- **Defensible ground:** the ECP-anchored, clinical-comfort quadrant, with the only standing membership. The map places MyACUVUE at 2 on the clinical-to-appearance axis and 8 on the transactional-to-ECP-anchored axis (ranges 1 to 4 and 7 to 9; Medium). Alcon is its nearest neighbour on structure but thinner in consumer presence (Low).
- **A claim of "membership value" is credible only as structure today.** Evidence does not yet support it as a lived experience (Medium).
- **Unclaimed ground (Low to Medium confidence):** first-wear confidence for new wearers (fear and handling are the stated barriers and no brand owns them) and continuity for existing wearers (reminders, history; users ask, no brand offers, but online retailers already message on reorder).
- **Contested ground:** comfort (all four brands claim it), price (premium against promotion and cross-border), and myopia control (CooperVision ahead on evidence).
- **Olens's territory** is not shared except through ACUVUE DEFINE; evidence for an ACUVUE opening there is Low.
- Of the eighteen segment-by-stage cells, seven are marked insufficient evidence (including most considerer and new-wearer cells), one is not applicable, and none is rated above Medium (see report section 9b).

## Three biggest uncertainties

1. **Where registrations are actually lost** (channel leakage, app verification, store binding, or staff not scanning). Needs J&J funnel and purchase-channel data. Until then H4 stays Mixed.
2. **Whether retailer incentives and behaviour work against registration** (H3). No ECP or chain voice exists. Nearly all ECP-influence evidence is non-Singapore and industry-funded.
3. **How Singapore consumers perceive the five brands.** The map reflects brand promise and structure, not perception. The barrier ranking by segment is inferred from partly non-Singapore evidence, and marketplaces (H5) are unobserved.

## What this analysis cannot tell us
Sales, share by brand, volume, funnel conversion, new-wearer retention in Singapore, the size of any segment, and competitor programme uptake. H1 rests on one paywalled source covering lenses plus solutions.

**Next evidence most worth obtaining:** (1) J&J CRM and app analytics; (2) ECP and chain-buyer interviews with outlet-level scan data; (3) the barrier-attribution survey with brand-image items; (4) Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop brand-store capture; (5) a Singapore new-wearer and parent sample.
