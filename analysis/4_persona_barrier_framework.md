# Persona and Barrier Framework

One profile per segment, built only from evidence that survived validation (see [2_deep_dive_report.md](2_deep_dive_report.md)). Every line carries a confidence rating and IDs resolving to [00_source_log.md](00_source_log.md). "Non-SG" marks behavioural evidence from outside Singapore. A stage with no corroborated evidence says so. These profiles describe segments, not individuals, and are not sized.

Confidence rubric: High = two or more independent source types including a primary or authoritative one, recent, no unresolved conflict; Medium = two sources of the same type, or one primary source uncorroborated, or minor conflicts; Low = single secondary source, dated, anecdotal, inferred or conflicted.

## Persona 1: Existing wearers

**Who.** People already wearing lenses, mostly dailies or bi-weekly; includes astigmatic and presbyopic wearers moving to premium dailies. Includes lapsed wearers who could resume (R1-C-26). Not separately identified: age, share of wearers.

**Needs (corroborated)**
- Comfort through the day; dryness and discomfort are the leading reasons established wearers stop (Medium; R1-C-24, C-25, non-SG with one Singapore dry-eye survey; SC-KP threads on comfort and dryness).
- Total cost across channels, not just brand price (Medium; R4-C-06, C-12; R3-C-21; SC-RD price questions).
- Easy reorder: users ask for replacement-date tracking and order or prescription history (Low to Medium; R4-C-14, SC-APP 2018 and 2020).
- A lens that works for astigmatism or presbyopia; those wearers drop out more when vision is the issue (Medium, non-SG; R1-C-14).

**Barriers by stage**

| Stage | Barrier or friction | Conf. | IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | No corroborated barrier; product news draws attention (Low) | Low | SC-YT, SC-GT, R2-C-12 |
| Engagement | Registration gated by Singapore mobile number and OTP, NRIC/FIN, named residency or pass types, Singapore store region (mechanics **High**). Reported OTP, login and update failures (Medium for existence, Low for prevalence) | High / Medium | R4-C-03, C-04, C-05, C-15; R2-C-14; R3-C-14; SC-APP |
| Consideration | Compares cost across Singapore stores, online retailers and Johor Bahru; sees ACUVUE as comfortable but expensive | Medium | R4-C-06; R3-C-21; SC-RD; SC-KP |
| Trial | No corroborated evidence for this segment | n/a | none |
| Purchase | Points earned only at the chosen store with staff scanning; purchases elsewhere earn nothing | High (mechanic); Medium (behaviour) | R4-C-11, C-12; R3-C-14 |
| Repeat / Retention | Discomfort or dryness; store-binding and points freezing; app seen as a "points tracker"; retailer-owned loyalty competes for attention; price-driven re-use of dailies | Medium / Medium / Low / Low / Low | R1-C-24, C-22; R4-C-13, C-14; R3-C-09, C-10 |

**Language used**
- On ACUVUE: "comfortable", "expensive", "price increased again", "$420+" (Low; R4 section 9c; SC-KP "expensive for daily wear").
- On the app: "points tracker", "useless", "lousy", "broken", "frustrated", "can't get OTP", "points are freeze" (Medium; SC-APP, R4 section 9c).
- On channel: "cheaper in JB", "order via WhatsApp", "price difference is crazy" (Medium; R4-C-06, C-12).

**Channel touchpoints (observed, not recommended)**
- Optical chains and independent opticians (Capitol, Owndays, Better Vision, W Optics and others) (R3-C-04, C-15); MyACUVUE app and PRO app (R4); Johor Bahru opticians via WhatsApp; online lens retailers with SMS, WhatsApp or email reorder (R4-C-16); HardwareZone, Lemon8, Reddit, KiasuParents for peer talk (R4, SC-RD, SC-KP); YouTube for product videos (SC-YT).

**Open questions.** Share buying outside participating stores; OTP success rate; whether 2026 app updates fixed failures; Singapore dropout reasons.

## Persona 2: New wearers

**Who.** People in the first year of lens wear, often fitted at a chain, often starting with cosmetic or clear dailies. Not separately identifiable in scraped data. No Singapore retention data.

**Needs (corroborated)**
- Reassurance about infection and handling; confidence putting lenses in and taking them out (Medium; R4-C-08 Singapore posts; R1-C-06, C-14, C-15, non-SG).
- A fitting and teaching that feels proper (Low; R4-C-10, R3-C-02).
- Trial lenses and clear care instructions; the strongest US practice behaviours reported were trial lenses for new prescriptions (69%) and ensuring care is understood (67%) (Low; R1-C-17, press summaries only).
- A lens that is tried again if the first fails: 71% of UK dropouts had no alternative tried (Medium, non-SG; R1-C-14).

**Barriers by stage**

| Stage | Barrier or friction | Conf. | IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | No corroborated evidence for Singapore | n/a | none |
| Engagement | No corroborated evidence of how new wearers reach the app | n/a | none |
| Consideration | Fear of infection and of the lens "rolling behind" the eye | Medium | R4-C-08; R1-C-06 |
| Trial | Handling and orientation difficulty after teaching; free teaching tied to a 2-box minimum at one chain (single source family); thin fitting | Medium / Low / Low | R4-C-08, C-09, C-10; R3-C-02 |
| Purchase | Own-brand clear lenses at about S$1 per lens at one chain against about S$2.03 for ACUVUE 1-Day Moist; welcome rewards from S$20 to S$60 | Medium (price points) / Medium (range) | R3-C-01, C-04; R2-C-13; R4 section 5 |
| Repeat / Retention | About one in four drop out in the first year in UK studies; reasons vision (26% to 41%), discomfort (14% to 36%), handling (15% to 25%); the first weeks matter most (non-SG) | Medium | R1-C-14, C-23, K2 |

**Language used**
- "Scared", "eye infection videos", "roll behind", "intimidating", "struggling" (orientation), "red or irritated" (Medium; R4-C-08, section 9c).
- Value framing: free teaching "minimum 2 boxes" (Low; R4-C-09).

**Channel touchpoints (observed)**
- Fitting at optical chains (Owndays and others) (R3-C-02, R4-C-09); Lemon8 first-timer posts (R4); free trial lenses and referral offers via ACUVUE and chains (R3-C-15); Instagram ads for direct-to-consumer brands (R4-C-02, Low).

**Open questions.** Singapore first-year retention; who starts wearing without an ECP; what share of new wearers register.

## Persona 3: Active considerers

Three sub-groups with different evidence. **(a) Glasses-only wearers considering lenses; (b) cosmetic-lens considerers; (c) parents of children with myopia** (outside the three defined segments; tagged "segment not identifiable, parent").

**Needs (corroborated)**
- (a) Freedom from glasses and a professional who raises lenses as an option (Medium; R1-C-02, C-07, C-08; non-SG, industry-commissioned).
- (b) Appearance and a natural look at a very low price per unit (Medium; R2-C-06; V4, V7 scraped; R4 grid; resellers' marketing UNVERIFIED).
- (c) Safety and a clear explanation of why myopia management matters; a recommendation from the eye professional (Medium, mostly non-SG; R1-C-09, C-10, C-11, C-34).

**Barriers by stage**

| Stage | Barrier or friction | Conf. | IDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | (a) Few are told they are candidates (non-SG, industry-commissioned). (b) Content is decorative; 3.5% of TikTok lens videos mentioned seeing an eye professional (2022). (c) Information comes from providers | Medium / Low / Low | R1-C-02; R3-C-24; R4-C-07 |
| Engagement | No corroborated evidence | n/a | none |
| Consideration | (a) Fear of touching the eye, handling anxiety, no ECP prompt. (b) Low barrier: lenses are widely listed at low prices outside ECPs. (c) Safety and hygiene concerns, ECP default to spectacles or atropine, cost | Medium / Medium / Medium | R1-C-06, C-07; R4-C-08; SC-PRD; R3-C-20; R1-C-09, C-10, C-11 |
| Trial | No corroborated Singapore evidence. Myopia-control consensus recommends trial-lens fitting (sponsor-funded) | Medium (consensus) | R1-C-34 |
| Purchase | (b) Cosmetic purchases at S$5 to S$36 per listing unit sit outside the ECP channel. (c) Myopia-control lens cost one report S$640 (pre-window) | Low | SC-PRD; R4 voice table |
| Repeat / Retention | Not applicable (no purchase yet) | n/a | none |

**Language used**
- (b) "Korean", "natural look", "K-beauty", "affordable", "$10 for monthly" (Low; mostly reseller and brand marketing, not consumer voice) (R4 section 9c; R3-C-20). Xiaohongshu themes: recommendation, value, price, comfort, colour, authenticity (Low; SC-XHS, n=68 posts).
- (c) "Slow progression", "Ortho-K", "MiSight", "LifeSG credits" (provider framing, Low; R4-C-07).

**Channel touchpoints (observed)**
- (a) Optical chains and ECPs (R1-C-07 non-SG). (b) Olens resellers, cross-border e-store, WhatsApp sellers, TikTok Shop and Instagram content (R2-C-25; SC-PRD; SC-YT; SC-XHS). (c) Clinics, SNEC and optical-association content, KiasuParents (R1-C-09; R4-C-07; SC-KP, 2023).

**Open questions.** Who the Singapore considerers are, how many go on to buy fitted lenses, whether cosmetic buyers ever cross into the ECP path (H5b), and Singapore parent decision drivers.

## What this framework cannot tell us
Segment sizes, how many people in each segment never reach registration, Singapore-specific retention, and how consumers (rather than brands) describe their own positioning of the five brands. The language lists are drawn from small, partly dated or inferred-date samples and from retailer marketing as well as consumer posts.
