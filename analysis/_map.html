MyACUVUE positioning map, Singapore

Five brands placed on two axes chosen from the evidence in four research runs and the scraped data, as at 1 October 2026.

Reading it. MyACUVUE and CooperVision share the clinical, ECP-anchored quadrant; CooperVision leans clinical (myopia control), MyACUVUE leans structured relationship (membership). Alcon is its nearest neighbour on structure. Bausch + Lomb sits in the middle on price-led bundles. Olens holds the opposite corner.

Why these axes. They separate the five brands most clearly and match the segment structure. Monthly cost was rejected because price follows modality more than brand; prescription complexity because it describes portfolio breadth with little placement evidence; loyalty depth alone because it moves with the ECP axis. This map shows brand promise and structure. No Singapore consumer perception data exist, so a consumer survey should test it. IDs resolve in the merged source log.
