## Confidence reconciliation notes
- **H4 Medium rather than High** despite High-confidence mechanics: the friction claim relies on one consumer source (app reviews, shared by R4 and SC-APP) and a conflict about whether it is the key drop-off.
- **H1 Medium rather than High:** one syndicated source; no Singapore second source.
- **H2 Low:** a conflict (growing versus falling volume) with a plausible cause (measure and hub effect); ranges shown, not averaged.
- No verdict was reached on any hypothesis by averaging conflicting sources.

## What this table cannot tell us
It cannot say where registrations are lost, whether retailers' bundle behaviour affects them, or what happens on marketplaces. Closing these needs J&J CRM and app analytics, ECP and chain-buyer interviews, and marketplace capture including Shopee.
