# SG contact-lens dashboard: standards

Streamlit dashboard in `Dashboard/`, scrapers and tagging scripts in `Scripts/`, desk research in `analysis/`. Project background is in `context.md` and `data.md`. Focal brand is Acuvue; peers are Alcon, Bausch & Lomb, CooperVision and Olens. The goal (context.md) is MyACUVUE registration 7% to 14% through WhatsApp: every page is read against what it says about the barrier to registering and the message to send.

## Structure: HK tab order, nested sub-tabs

An **Executive read** (`answer_page.exec_read`, a collapsed-by-default expander under the headline strip, above the tabs) carries the answer first: a one-line bottom line with the numbers under it, what is new and what is confirmed, what to send first (chart, messages, the Repeat/Retention line). It states no implication and no need for J&J data: everyone knows this is EBI insight only. Nine tabs follow in the HK dashboard's story order, with nested sub-tabs (the data first, the detail last): **Brand Overview** (competitors & category), **Brand Health** (`brand_market_page.py`; the tested channel gap leads, the composite score and trend follow), **Reviews & Sentiment** (New Wearers first, then Review / Sentiment Intelligence), **Social Signals** (XHS, Reddit, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook), **Journey & App** (`barriers_page.py`: Journey, App reviews, Message), **Retail & Promotions** (Google Maps, Facebook retailers), **Research & Triangulation** (desk research vs scraped data, Barriers (framework barriers on Acuvue complaints), category users & positioning with the Stage 2 hypotheses below it, **Key findings** (`answer_page.py`) first: how sure we are, the barrier-to-message-to-internal-data table, supporting facts) and **Trends & Demand**, **Data Notes** (coverage & method, data notes). Journey & App and Retail & Promotions are SG-only; Reddit takes HK's LIHKG slot. The story pages read only `Dashboard/voice_data.py`, so a number means the same thing on every page. Sub-tabs are built in `app._subparts` (`st.tabs` with `on_change="rerun"`); a tab's body runs only when it is selected (`top.open`). Story pages keep their numbered sections and their own `ui.nav` menu inside one sub-tab. Structure was changed only: page contents follow HK later.

- **Say each number once:** the six headline cards under the banner (data points, net sentiment, complaints above peers, app reviews, search interest, data window) are the only place those figures appear as cards. The Executive read has the bottom line, no tiles; Key findings holds the evidence and the barrier-to-message table. Supporting-fact cards leave out the app and demand figures.
- **Page shape (HK pattern):** each tab opens with the data, as in the HK dashboard (`Web scrapper/app.py`): HK's blocks in HK order, then SG-only blocks after them. No bottom-line card or `ui.pyramid` on a page; the Executive read above the tabs is the only bottom line. Every finding on it and on Key findings carries a **New** or **Confirms** tag, set against the hypotheses in `context.md` and the desk research (J&J can re-tag against what the team already knew). Pages with no data on lens buying are not kept: Price Intelligence, the Catalog Explorer tab (store ranking, brand protection) and RevitaLens pack prices were removed, and the HK gap pills with no SG data (Price vs. Sentiment, External Validation, Purchasing Trend, Before vs After), Share of Voice (home: Brand Overview), Barrier Matches (now in Barriers) and Stage 2 bridge (now in Research Findings) are retired. Numbered sections whose title leads with the finding (HK titles are neutral, SG titles state the finding), each chart under a one-line takeaway, tables collapsed, a short limits note last. Every line is crisp and carries only what the reader needs. Each number has one home page (see `docs/dashboard_summary.md`, overlaps list); other pages link to it.
- **Keep only data about the main product:** the product is contact lenses (Acuvue and its peers) and the question is MyACUVUE registration. A chart, table or data point that is not about lenses, or does not bear on the barrier to registering or the message to send, is removed, not kept as context. Example: solution price per 100 mL was dropped, because the HSA bars online lens sales and solution price says nothing about lens buying. Check this before adding any new data point.
- **Charts shown, tables on a click:** a chart is always visible; its table sits behind `ui.show_data(...)`; the items behind a bar open with `ui.plot(..., select=True)` + `ui.picked(ev)` + `ui.items_panel(vd.view(items))` (text, channel, date, link).
- **Proof at the foot of every page (as in HK):** a page ends with the evidence behind its numbers, in a closed expander with a word search and a link on every row. Voice items: `ui.read_comments(items, title, key)` (channel filter too). Listings, posts, videos and reviews: `ui.read_proof(df, title, cols, key)`. Pass what the page counted (brand pool, Acuvue complaint items, store reviews, the channel's posts and videos, priced listings), so a reader can check any number by reading the source. Pages with no raw evidence (Data Notes, Demand Signals' search index, the Stage 2 and desk-research views) have none; a page that already lists raw items keeps its own list.
- **One consistent frame across channels:** one item table, one tagger, one scale. Five lenses, never pooled: consumer voice (the brand pool, the only lens that compares brands), category voice (lens comments that name no brand: themes only), owned experience (the app), retail experience (Google Maps and retailer pages) and brand broadcast (own posts, ads). A sixth, **reference voice** (segment-study YouTube and TikTok comments, country unknown, `segment_items_sg.db` and `segment_tags_sg.db`, which also hold the 91 strictly on-topic Singapore Reddit posts of the segment study, read apart as "seen in Singapore"), is kept outside `voice_items`: it shows what viewers elsewhere ask and fear, as counts, themes and quotes only, and never enters the pool, a rate or a Singapore figure (read on New Wearers; label "not Singapore"). Brands are only compared on channels where both sides reach 15 items; "Acuvue vs peers" says which peer brand supplies most of the peer items (on YouTube it is Olens).
- **Journey stage** has two sources. The main tagger gives a stage for only about 40% of items, so that is never shown as a per-item percentage; a channel's role (`voice_data.CHANNEL_ROLE`, from context.md) is shown as the channel-role map. The journey chart (first section of Journey & App) uses `Scripts/tag_journey_stage.py` (`journey_stage_sg.db`, `voice_data.friction`): complaint items only, counts per stage stacked by source, never rates. The app is placed at Trial and Repeat by what it is, so a Trial peak is partly the app; the "Acuvue consumers only" view reads the stage from the comment alone.

## Labels: one standard for every channel

Chosen on three tests: it serves the registration and WhatsApp question, the evidence is deep enough to read, and it can be reproduced. No per-source label is used on the story pages.

- **Sentiment:** `Scripts/tag_voice_items.py` gives every item in `voice_items_sg.db` one sentiment (positive, neutral, mixed, negative toward the brand named first), 0 to 2 themes each with its own polarity, a journey stage and a content type, from one model prompt (OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini`, about $0.04 per 1,000 items) into `voice_tags_sg.db`. Scopes: `pool`, `lenses`, `category`, `all`; pilot first (`python tag_voice_items.py`), then `--mode full --scope <scope> --confirm`. The dashboard keeps only the latest `taxonomy_version`. Changing `THEMES` or the prompt changes the version: re-tag everything (about $0.11 for the 2,773 lens items, plus $0.01 for category voice).
- **Eight themes** (the tagging layer, source of truth `THEMES` in `Dashboard/theme_tags.py`): Comfort & product, Price & value, Look & colour, Trust & authenticity, Access & availability, Fitting & guidance, Loyalty & app (points, rewards, the app and how it works, registration, OTP, marketing and unsubscribing), Service (store or customer service, not the app itself).
- **Four decision groups** (what headlines report; `theme_tags.GROUPS`): **Product & look** (Comfort, Look), **Price, access & trust** (Price, Access, Trust), **Guidance & service** (Fitting, Service), **Loyalty & app**. Each group has one owner and one kind of message (`method_page.GROUP_OWNER`). Do not invent a new category list and do not keyword-match a private one per page.
- **Praise vs complaint** comes from the polarity the model gave that theme (`voice_data.group_labels`), not from the theme and not from the item's overall sentiment.
- **Complaint** = one definition on every page: an item that is negative or mixed on a theme group (`voice_data.complaints`). The per-source barrier flags are retired.
- **Barrier labels** (12, `barrier_taxonomy.TYPES`, rolled up through `THEME_OF`) are detail under the groups: the app issue bars, the WhatsApp map and the desk-research check. `barrier_taxonomy.classify(text)` returns the model's label once the text is tagged by `Scripts/theme_tag_sg.py` (`theme_tags_sg.db`), else a keyword draft. Do not add a second list.
- **Barrier charts show the framework barrier labels, not Product & look:** the Barrier section of Research & Triangulation (`barriers_page.render_barrier`, and any chart of what blocks registration) uses the desk-research framework barriers (`barrier_taxonomy`, as on Triangulation: price, loyalty, registration/login, app utility, messaging/privacy, fear/handling, professional guidance), read on complaint items. **Product & look (Comfort, Look, "Product experience") is left out of those charts**: it is already the top complaint in the headline strip and no WhatsApp message moves it. Say the count set aside in the section subtitle. Acuvue vs peers is not charted on these barriers while Acuvue's consumer complaints on them are under 15; use Acuvue's own complaints (consumers who name it plus the app).
- **Desk research** (`analysis/4_category_user_barrier_framework.md`) is data in `Dashboard/framework_check.py`; Triangulation reads it against the shared labels. The separate Prompt B comment base and the Prompt D per-brand prose are retired.

## Reading rules for any brand comparison

- **Net sentiment** = % positive minus % negative; neutral and mixed stay in the base. A pooled gap is also shown re-weighted to one channel mix (`voice_data.standardised`), because brands are strong on different channels.
- **Display limits for thin data** (constants in `Dashboard/ebi.py`; change them there, not per page):
  - **Rate or percentage: base of 15 items** (`MIN_N`). Under it show a dash or the count only. Leave out a row or column that no brand reaches 15 on, and say so in a note.
  - **One exception to the 15 rule: the Brand Health composite trend** (`brand_health_page._trend`). Points under 15 items are kept and drawn hollow (`ebi.is_thin`), and the caption counts them; read as direction only. Grains: Rolling 3 months, Quarter (default), Year. Do not copy this to other charts.
  - **Theme or bar drawn in a chart: 10 comments** (`MIN_COUNT`). Under it the bar is not drawn and appears only as a count in a note. Bars of 10 to 29 are hatched (directional).
  - **"X differs from Y" claim: 15 items across both groups, plus a significance test, plus the channel check.** Grades: Not readable, No gap (the interval holds zero), B (a real gap seen in one channel), A (the same gap in two channels with 15+ on both sides). A headline may not name the largest gap unless it passes the first two checks, and says which channel carries it.
- **Every chart has a sample-size strip** (`ui.plot(..., bases=, noun=)` or `ui.n_strip`).
- **Acuvue is the highlighted brand in any chart that shows brands:** Acuvue in its brand colour, peers in one neutral grey; its label and number in bold; where the chart has rows or columns, a light tint behind Acuvue's. No arrows or callouts (clutter). Where peers need their own colours (trend lines), Acuvue gets the thicker line and the bold legend entry.
- **Like-for-like:** brands are only compared on sources that cover them. The app (Acuvue only) and retail reviews are kept apart from the brand pool.
- **Giveaway entries are not opinions:** a comment under a contest post is written to win, so it is left out of every pool and sentiment figure. Instagram: `sg_common.is_contest_caption`; Facebook: the scraper's `is_contest_or_spam` flag. Pages say how many were removed.
- **Say how it was labelled:** the Data & method tab shows the tag version and item counts; a page that shows themes says they are model-tagged.

## Dashboard summary: one line per chart, kept current

`docs/dashboard_summary.md` is the map of what each page and chart shows, so a number or finding sits on one page only and the story keeps the HK order (data first, conclusion last). Read it before adding or moving anything on a page; update it in the same change whenever a page, chart, card or headline is added, changed, moved or removed.

- One block per tab > sub-tab, in tab order. Per chart one line: `chart: what it plots | key number or finding | source`. Per card or headline the same. No prose, no methods (those live in this file and `context.md`), target under 250 lines.
- Before adding a figure, search the file: if it already appears, link to that page instead of repeating it. Flag overlaps in an "Overlaps to resolve" list at the bottom.
- Page review is one page at a time: when the user finishes checking a page, record its final state there.

## Working notes

- Run the dashboard from `Dashboard/`: `python -m streamlit run app.py`. Headless checks use `streamlit.testing.v1.AppTest` with `session_state["main_tabs"]` set to the tab name. Restart the server after editing modules.
- Files use mixed line endings (most CRLF, some LF); keep each file's endings when editing.
- **Source ledger (`Dashboard/data_ledger.py`):** EBI shows the client everything collected, so the sidebar headline and Data Notes count every body of data from its own file: the four pool lenses, reference voice (segment study) and AI answers make the item total (13,864 on 2026-10-28), with weekly search-interest points on a separate line (index values, not written items). Pooling for analysis is separate and unchanged (the 935-item brand pool, reference voice never in a rate). Added today comes from collected-at stamps (blank where a source keeps none). The sidebar stamp is the newest build across sources; a raw table whose row count differs from `voice_items`, a reference export newer than its build, or reference items without tags shows "Out of date". After adding data, rebuild and tag the matching table (`build_voice_items.py` + `tag_voice_items.py`; `tag_segment_comments.py --build` + `--tag --confirm`), check the ledger status and run `consistency_check.py`. A new source gets a ledger row, not its own sidebar line.
- Every page must show the same number for the same thing. Per-channel pages take their sentiment from `voice_data.unify` (matched by each scraper row's own id), not from the scraper's own column. After changing any loader, label or filter, run `python consistency_check.py` from `Dashboard/`; it exits 1 if a channel page, the pool, the desk-research channel table or the passport disagree.
- A pointer to another tab is a button, not a sentence: `ui.goto(tab, label, key=, section=)` (secondary style; `section` picks a Research & Triangulation pill). Keys must be unique per page.
- Do not commit unless asked.
